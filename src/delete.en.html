Delete your Court Rhythm account

Court Rhythm app by BEMZ Europe sp. z o.o.

The short version

  • You can delete your account yourself in the app: Settings → Delete my account.
  • No longer have the app? Email {EMAIL} from the address linked to your account.
  • Your account and the personal data linked to it are deleted. Court information you sent can stay on the map, without any link to you.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Court Rhythm and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

2. Delete your account without the app

Write to {EMAIL} from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my Court Rhythm account". If you signed in with Google (or with Apple, when an iPhone version is released), use that account's email address. If we need to confirm that the account is yours, we will ask. We delete the account within one month at the latest and confirm by email.

3. What we delete

  • Your account: email address and the link to your Google sign-in (or Apple sign-in, when an iPhone version is released).
  • Your profile: nickname, sports, skill levels and year of birth.
  • Your check-ins from the last 60 days.
  • Your favourite and hidden courts, and the players you blocked.
  • Your consent records (what you agreed to and when).
  • Reports you sent. Reports other players made about you are kept without any link to your account until the report is closed, then for 6 months; longer only while a case or claim about it is open.

Your position is never stored, so there is no location history to delete.

Court photos are taken by our team only, with only buildings in the frame. The app has no access to your pictures.

4. What may be kept

  • Court information you sent (for example a court's sports, opening hours or condition) can stay on the map, but the link to you is removed.
  • Older check-ins: after 60 days the link to you is removed; anonymous counts stay (court, sport and hour only).
  • Data we must keep by law, only for as long as the law requires.
  • Backups are overwritten on their normal schedule.

5. Delete some data and keep your account

  • Change your nickname, sports or skill levels in Settings → Profile.
  • Withdraw location consent in Settings → Location consent.
  • Download a copy of the personal data in your account in Settings → Export my data. Some information, for example reports other people made about you, is not part of the export.
  • To have other specific data deleted, write to {EMAIL}.

More details are in our Privacy policy.
