Every court, alive.One tap away.

Court Rhythm is a mobile app for basketball, football and volleyball. It maps public courts and shows, in real time, where people are playing, so you know before you leave home whether there's a game.

Our motto: Less guessing, more playing.

The courts are there. The games are hard to find.

Poland has tens of thousands of public and school courts. Most players know the two or three closest to home, and still can't tell whether anyone will be there.

  • Courts are invisible

    Most players only know the few courts nearest to home. The rest of the neighbourhood's courts go unused.

  • Nobody knows who's there

    People turn up to an empty court, or skip going because they assume no one will be there to play with.

  • Games are scattered

    Pick-up games, tournaments and open hours are announced across group chats, posters and word of mouth.

How Court Rhythm works

Open the app, look at the map, go where the game is.

  1. Find a court

    See basketball, football and volleyball courts near you on one map, filtered by sport.

  2. See who's playing

    Each court shows how many people are there right now, and when it's usually busy.

  3. Check in when you arrive

    Tap check-in at the court. Our server confirms you're really there, then forgets your position.

Privacy is part of the design

  • Location only while the app is openWe never ask for background location. Without it you can still browse the map.
  • Your position becomes a yes or noThe server checks whether you are at the court, then discards the position. Exact coordinates are never stored or shown to anyone.
  • Profiles only from three players upBelow three people at a court, others see a number, nothing more.
  • Your age stays privateNobody sees your age. We only check that you are 16 or over.
  • Hosted in the European UnionApp data is stored in the EU, under the GDPR (RODO).
